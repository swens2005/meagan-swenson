Case 01 · 2011–2016, back again in 2026

Wisconsin Ignition Interlock Device

Processing Application and client Portal for an ignition-interlock provider

WIID installs and services ignition-interlock devices. Each client signs up, signs several agreements, has a device installed and pays for it over time. We moved that whole process online, in one system for clients and staff.

  • For clients: online registration, tutorial videos to watch and sign off, and service, lease and install agreements filled in online with printable PDFs
  • For staff: a portal with device inventory (in stock, in use, removed), vehicle transfers, scheduling and reports, including collections and device recovery
  • Payments: clients synced to QuickBooks and reconciled by device ID, recurring card payments through Forte and ACH bank payments through FTNI
  • Migration: moved from Classic ASP and Microsoft Access to PHP and MySQL while the business kept running on it

105 work orders and about 1,700 hours over five years. In 2026 WIID asked me back to rebuild the Processing Application and the Portal. This system holds personal and legal records, so I describe it in words only: no screenshots.

Case 02 · 2018–2019

Dealer ordering platform

One B2B parts store, launched three times: CNH Hardware, AGCO Supply and Trinity

A parts distributor needed a fast way for its dealers to find and reorder hardware. We designed and built a dealer store, then reused the same platform for two more brands with their own designs and catalogs.

  • Barcode scanning with the phone's camera: scan a part, get the SKU, add it to the cart
  • Category drill-down with sidebar filters, keyword and SKU search, and ordering without leaving the page
  • Dealer accounts with order history, one-click reorder (unavailable parts marked), wish lists and carts kept per user
  • Imports and exports for products, dealers and orders; payment gateway and UPS shipping rates; recommended products on every product page
  • Later: products assigned to specific customers, so each dealer sees only their own items

About 640 hours across the three launches, tested in Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari.

Case 03 · 2019–2020

BackTrack Video

A marketplace for security-camera footage of accidents and incidents

Attorneys, insurance adjusters and investigators need video of an incident; homeowners and businesses have cameras that caught it. BackTrack Video connects the two, and buyers can request a video anonymously.

  • Camera owners register cameras on a Google map; buyers search by location and request a video
  • Checkout through PayPal, video upload through Cloudinary, and order emails and text messages to both sides
  • Incident alerts: buyers sign up for new incident videos within a radius, by incident type, by email or text
  • A white-label portal for companies: their own logo and colors, sub-accounts with permissions, and order reports (daily to year-to-date) with Excel export
  • Shared code modules for buyers and sellers, used by both the public site and the portal, plus system documentation and a flow chart

Case 04 · 2015–2021

Diggers Hotline

Rescuing a hacked site for Wisconsin's 811 one-call center, then building its portals

Diggers Hotline came to us with a hacked website that Google was warning visitors about. We cleaned it up, rebuilt it and stayed for six years.

  • Rescue: removed the compromised code and rebuilt the site, content and forms as a responsive site on our CMS
  • Security: access-log reports with IP blocking in the CMS, an hourly check that flags any added or changed file for approval, and nightly off-site backups to Amazon S3, kept for 90 days
  • Redesign: three design directions, a search across the site's data and templates for videos and PDFs
  • Portals: a complaint and enforcement portal with verified accounts, file uploads (with type, size and count limits), statuses and admin review, plus an automatic archive of old submissions

Case 05 · 2009–2012

MyWindowBuilder

Instant online estimates for replacement windows, for HomeSealed

HomeSealed wanted homeowners to measure their windows, build them online and get a price on the spot, with no in-home appointment needed.

  • A window builder where every choice updates the price and the options that follow: some installation types only appear for wood-and-aluminum double-hungs, some shapes only for vinyl
  • Combined units (a picture window between two double-hungs, for example) priced per window in the unit
  • Around 300 window images, one for every style, color and grid combination
  • Each estimate emailed to the homeowner and to HomeSealed, with coupons per product line and appointment booking
  • In 2012, rebuilt in PHP with prices kept in a database, so HomeSealed could update them without a developer

Case 06 · 2012–2013

Crossfade.fm

A songwriting competition: fans write the lyrics, bands write the songs

Anyone could submit a "song bite": a line of lyrics, a scrap of poetry or a mood. Bands picked one, recorded a song around it and uploaded it, and the community voted alongside the judges for cash prizes.

  • Song-bite and track submission forms, with the upload size checked in the browser before sending and again on the server
  • Rounds with voting, judging and a winners page, plus newsletters for each round in MailChimp
  • A later rebuild added user accounts, permissions and a login
